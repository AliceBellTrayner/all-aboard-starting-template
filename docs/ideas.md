# Team Ideas

## Offer

- Unconditional offer which triggers the onboarding

## Pre day 1

- Buddy chat function - dating app style match for job family buddy based on interests; Team buddy is chosen by LM.
- Encourage team to meet new starter prior to first day
- 2 different buddies - 1 team buddy and 1 job family (recent joiner)
- Before day 1, Line Manager checklist: buddies are assigned, devices chosen, ID badge requested, 3 months goal set, welcome pack has been sent.
- My Devices page - app recommends choice of devices based on job family so colleague can pick what they prefer (Mac or Windows, etc.)
- Ability to upload pass with instructions and requirements
- Parking, transport
- Tips to buddies and LMs about integration, ie. adding colleague to the team site and use their linkedin pic, for example
- Clear goals, set goals in pre-boarding

## First day

- Line manager/buddy/new starter versions of the app
- Personalisation as default (what channels, apps, etc.)
- Accessibility

- AI agent function for weekend queries (where do we source the info from?)

## 60-90 days

- First check-in review goals

## Open questions / risks

- What defines a 'buddy'? Same team, same area, same job family? Same platform, same job title

## Pain Points

- Deliver choreography across dozens of journeys during Day 1-90 - feedback from 1500 verbatims is clear - new joiners do not want 'more content; they want better choreography and a guided journey
- Deliver operational enablement as well as social welcome
- Preboarding that drives our EVP manifesto, inspiring new hires to bring their Horse Power for a fast start. Of 20 new starter metrifcs, preboarding experience is the 18th lowest score at 78.8
- Simplify compliance to allow managers to focus on connection and culture buildings - when line managers play an active role in onboarding, new hire satisfaction increases by 3.5 times.
- Prepare new-in leaders to build our future - 19th lowest scoring metric at 74.4.
- about 1 in 5 people leave within 12 months of joining lloyds - 8% overall are regrettable attrition
