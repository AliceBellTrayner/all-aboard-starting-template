# Goal
- To create an onboarding app for new colleagues based on the following ideas.

## UX Ideas
- Line manager/buddy/new starter experiences of the app
- Timeline like Duolingo, winding path on a field with black horses like LBG's Cancara with horse riding jumps in between with circles that tick complete once done
- App Store within the app with description of what each one does: Skillsoft; Kitchen2Go; Eptura Engage; Headspace; Scottish Widows Pension; Workday; Microsoft 365 Suite; OneHub; Amex GBT. App store could be tailored based on job family, ie. Pluralsight for engineers; Power BI/Tableau for data analysts

## Integration
- Azure

## Workflow
## Stage 1: Offer

- Unconditional offer which triggers the onboarding
- Email with app link is sent when unconditional offer is sent

## Stage 2: Pre day 1 (onboarding, vetting, getting to know team in advance, notice periods - this could be anything from 1 month to 6 months before first day)

- Buddy chat function - dating app style match for job family buddy based on interests; Team buddy is chosen by LM.
- Personalisation as default (what channels, apps, etc.)
- Accessibility
- Encourage team to meet new starter prior to first day - 'Your manager and team buddy want to meet you! Let's meet on your own terms. Would you prefer a virtual coffee meet up or an in-person meeting?'
- 2 different buddies - 1 team buddy and 1 job family (recent joiner)
- Before day 1, Line Manager checklist: buddies are assigned, devices chosen, ID badge requested, 3 months goal set, welcome pack has been sent.
- My Devices page - app recommends choice of devices based on job family so colleague can pick what they prefer (Mac or Windows, etc.)
- Ability to upload pass with instructions and requirements
- Parking, transport (discount codes)
- Tips to buddies and LMs about integration, ie. adding colleague to the team site and use their linkedin pic, for example
- AI agent function for weekend queries (where do we source the info from?)
- CHatbot that acts as Prosper for onboarding journey (HR related policies) - pay, rewards, benefits, pensions, Validium (EAP), etc.
- Vetting complete triggers sharing of info such as team vision, mandatory training, start date and schedule of events
- 2 weeks before start date: 'Your start date is XXth MONTH - click here (Eptura Engage opens in separate app) to book your desk next to COLLEAGUE NAME.'

## Stage 3: First day
- Arranged tours
- Pick up kit
- Wider team meeting

## Stage 4: First week
- System access
- Initial training 


## Stage 5: First 30 days
- Clear goals, set goals
- Getting paid 'Your first pay is here, go to Workday to see your payslip and breakdown'

## Stage 6: 30-90 days

- First check-in review goals

## Open questions / risks

- What defines a 'buddy'? Same team, same area, same job family? Same platform, same job title

## Pain Points

- Deliver choreography across dozens of journeys during Day 1-90 - feedback from 1500 verbatims is clear - new joiners do not want 'more content; they want better choreography and a guided journey
- Deliver operational enablement as well as social welcome
- Preboarding that drives our EVP manifesto, inspiring new hires to bring their Horse Power for a fast start. Of 20 new starter metrifcs, preboarding experience is the 18th lowest score at 78.8
- Simplify compliance to allow managers to focus on connection and culture buildings - when line managers play an active role in onboarding, new hire satisfaction increases by 3.5 times.
- Prepare new-in leaders to build our future - 19th lowest scoring metric at 74.4.
- about 1 in 5 people leave within 12 months of joining lloyds - 8% overall are regrettable attrition
